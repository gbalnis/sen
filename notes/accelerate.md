# Accelerate

*December 19, 2025*

Forsgren, N., Humble, J., & Kim, G. (2018). *Accelerate: Building and Scaling High Performing Technology Organizations*. IT Revolution.

---

## Quick Summary of What the Book Covers

*Accelerate* presents findings from the DORA (DevOps Research and Assessment) team's multi-year research program. The book identifies the technical, process, and cultural practices that drive high-performing engineering organizations.

### Headline Findings

Elite performers:

- Deploy on demand, often multiple times per day
- Achieve lead times of less than one hour from commit to production
- Recover from failures in minutes rather than days
- Maintain dramatically lower change failure rates

The central question:

> What do elite engineering teams do differently, and how do those practices translate into business performance?

---

## Key Takeaways & Lessons Learned

### High-Performing Engineering Teams Look Different

High performance is not created by heroics or longer working hours.

It comes from:

- Systemic excellence
- Intentional engineering practices
- Purposeful organizational design

A critical insight is that speed and stability are not opposing goals. Well-designed systems can achieve both.

---

### Software Delivery Performance Is a Business Superpower

Research showed strong correlations between software delivery performance and organizational outcomes:

- Profitability
- Market share
- Productivity
- Customer satisfaction

DevOps is therefore a strategic business capability, not merely an engineering concern.

---

### Technical Practices Drive Results

High-impact practices include:

- Trunk-based development
- Automated testing
- Deployment pipelines
- Small, frequent changes
- Continuous Delivery
- Version control for everything
- Shift-left quality practices
- Architecting for team independence

Infrastructure, configuration, scripts, and environments should all be managed through version control.

---

### Architecture Determines Speed More Than Process

Elite organizations enable teams to:

- Deploy services independently
- Test in isolation
- Own code from commit through production
- Limit blast radius
- Minimize coordination overhead

The book reframes architecture as a primary organizational performance lever.

---

### Culture Isn't Fluff, It's Infrastructure

Drawing on Ron Westrum's research, Accelerate shows that culture predicts engineering outcomes.

Characteristics of high-performing cultures:

- Blameless postmortems
- Trust and psychological safety
- Open information flow
- Encouragement of responsible risk taking
- Learning rather than finger-pointing
- Transparent and asynchronous communication

Low-performing cultures tend to rely on fear, bureaucracy, and siloed knowledge.

---

### Measure What Matters: The DORA Metrics

The book popularized four key metrics:

1. Deployment Frequency
2. Lead Time for Changes
3. Change Failure Rate
4. Time to Restore Service

These metrics balance speed and quality while remaining simple and actionable.

---

### Lean Principles Amplify Everything

Important lean practices include:

- Reducing batch sizes
- Visualizing work
- Limiting work in progress (WIP)
- Eliminating handoffs
- Building quality in
- Making work observable

Lean thinking connects architecture, culture, tooling, and workflow into a coherent performance system.

---

### Transformations Must Respect Human Systems

Recommendations for successful change:

- Start with small wins
- Empower teams
- Avoid mandates
- Do not over-engineer process
- Lead with data
- Celebrate improvements
- Build momentum rather than compliance

DevOps is not something that can be installed. It is a capability that must be cultivated.

---

## Why Return to This Book?

- It is one of the most evidence-backed books on engineering performance.
- It provides a practical playbook for scaling technology organizations.
- The DORA metrics remain a durable framework for assessing delivery effectiveness.
- It demonstrates that elite engineering performance is achievable and measurable.
- It provides a blueprint for building fast, resilient, and sustainable delivery organizations.

---

## Final Take

*Accelerate* fundamentally changed how the industry thinks about software delivery performance. By combining rigorous research with practical guidance, it demonstrates that technical excellence, organizational culture, team architecture, and business outcomes are tightly connected.

The book provides a clear framework for building technology organizations that are simultaneously faster, safer, more resilient, and more effective.
